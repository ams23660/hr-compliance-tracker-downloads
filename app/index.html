AI research (free)

No free government database covers paid sick leave, family and medical leave, or final paycheck rules state by state. With an AI service turned on, the app reads each of your states' official labor department pages, has the AI summarize what they say, and keeps an answer only if the AI quotes a sentence that the app finds word for word on that official page. Answers show in Compare states marked “AI summary, verify”, with the quote and a link to the page. Each state is re-checked monthly.

The page text and your questions are sent to the AI service you pick; nothing about your company is sent. Google's free tier may use prompts to improve its products. Ollama keeps everything on this computer. AI summaries can be wrong: always open the source link before acting on one.

Mac Your computer

Download for Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) Download for Intel Mac

Not sure which Mac you have? Click the Apple menu, then About This Mac. "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple Silicon. "Processor: Intel" means Intel. Needs macOS 11 or later.

Installing on a Mac
  1. Open the downloaded file and drag HR Compliance Tracker into the Applications folder.
  2. Open it from Applications.
If your Mac says it can't check the app for malicious software: this app is not yet registered with Apple, so the first launch needs one extra step.
  1. Click Done (or OK) on the message.
  2. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to the message about HR Compliance Tracker and click Open Anyway.
  4. Enter your Mac password, then click Open. You only do this once.

Windows Your computer

Download for Windows 10 or 11
Installing on Windows
  1. Open the downloaded Setup file and click Install, then Finish. No administrator rights needed.
  2. If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears, click More info, then Run anyway.

iPhone and iPad

Use the free web version: the same laws, state comparisons and compliance checklist, refreshed every day.

Open the phone version
Put it on your Home Screen
  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Open the phone version.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

It then opens from its own icon, like an app. Your states, checklist and saved items stay on your phone. It also works on Android phones (Chrome menu, then Add to Home screen) and in any web browser.
